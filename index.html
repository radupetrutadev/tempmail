    Questions

    Is this a real inbox?

    Yes. The address accepts real SMTP mail from anywhere on the internet — it isn't a simulation. Delivery normally takes a few seconds.

    How long does the address last?

    It stays yours as long as you keep it in this browser — it's saved locally and restored when you come back. Messages themselves are held for roughly seven days by the mail backend, then dropped.

    Can I send email from it?

    No. Tempbox is receive-only, which is what keeps it useless to spammers. Reply links and confirmation codes work fine; outbound mail does not.

    Is it private?

    Anyone who knows or guesses the address can read its mail — random addresses are not secret. Never use a disposable address for banking, government, or account recovery. Remote images are blocked until you ask for them, so tracking pixels don't fire on open.

    What happens when I hit Delete?

    The address and everything in it are destroyed on the backend and a fresh address is issued. There's no undo.
